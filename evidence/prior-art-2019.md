# 2019 Rural Energy Programme — final report (excerpt)

## Component 2: Shared photovoltaic micro-grids

Between March and November 2019 the programme installed shared 40 kW photovoltaic
micro-grids with lithium battery storage on twelve alpine farm cooperatives in the
Vercors and Queyras valleys.

Each micro-grid is owned jointly by the cooperative members and is sized to cover
year-round demand, including winter heating loads that were previously served by
individual diesel generators. Members pay a per-kWh cooperative tariff instead of
buying diesel.

Measured outcome: winter diesel consumption on the twelve cooperatives fell by 81%
in the first year, and 214 member farms stopped operating their own generators.
